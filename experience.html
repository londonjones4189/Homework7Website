      A Closer Look

      Bose

      Last year, I spent six months at Bose as a Copywriter Co-op on the creative team in their Boston office. One of the highlights of my experience was contributing to the Bose x LISA campaign, where I developed copy across a variety of platforms, including web pages, emails, app push notifications, posters, billboards, pop-up content, product detail pages, and giveaway pages. I also had the opportunity to work on two additional campaign launches: the LISA x Petal Pink lookbook and the LISA x Lunar Blue launch. Through these projects, I learned how to adapt brand messaging across different channels while maintaining a consistent creative vision.

      IBM

      This past summer, I had the amazing opportunity to work at IBM’s Austin office as a Technical Writing Intern on the AI for Developers team. My internship project centered on DeckFlow which is a DJ software built using IBM’s agentic IDE. I contributed to the project by fixing bugs, restructuring code, developing new features, and building and writing its documentation site. Beyond development, I also created promotional content for DeckFlow featured on IBM’s THINK blog. A highlight of the experience was seeing DeckFlow used by a professional DJ at IBM’s TechXchange conference last month, bringing our work to a live audience.
