London Jones

Hi, I’m a fourth-year student at Northeastern University majoring in Computer Science and English with an analytics focus and minoring in Business Administration. Ever since I was little, I have loved telling stories. I remember writing my first one in my journal about a one-winged fairy named Cleo who lived on the island of Randomia. Looking back, I don't think much has changed. I still love creating worlds, exploring ideas, and finding ways to bring stories to life. The difference is that my storytelling now extends far beyond the pages of my journal.

From product management to software engineering to technical writing, every role has given me a different way to tell a story, whether through the experience of a product, a line of code, or a page of words. I love the intersection of creativity and technology and finding ways to make complex ideas accessible, engaging, and meaningful. I’m especially interested in how design and technology can shape the way people interact with the world around them.

I’m originally from Rochester, New York. When I'm not writing or coding, you'll probably find me watching video essays, hate-watching movies, listening to music, or buried in a book. I also love matcha, figure skating, and being a twin!
